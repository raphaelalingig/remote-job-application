# Campaign Intake Checklist

Collect only items not already available in the resume, tracker, files, or conversation.

## Required to Begin

- Current master resume or source resume file.
- Legal/preferred name, email, phone, city/country, and the contact details approved for applications.
- Target roles or permission to infer them from the resume.
- Fully remote requirement and countries/regions where the candidate may legally work.
- Work authorization and sponsorship requirement for each target region.
- Employment types accepted: permanent, contract, part-time, freelance, or temporary.
- Earliest start date or notice period.
- Jobs, companies, industries, schedules, or duties to exclude.

## Needed Before Relevant Applications

- Minimum base salary or acceptable range and currency; whether “negotiable” is allowed.
- Working hours, time zones, overnight/weekend shifts, travel, and relocation preferences.
- LinkedIn profile, portfolio, GitHub, website, certifications, and work samples to include.
- Accurate years of experience for frequently asked skills when the resume does not make this clear.
- Education details, graduation dates if required, licenses, and credential identifiers.
- Preferred answer or case-by-case handling for voluntary demographic/self-identification questions.
- Cover-letter preference: none, short, or full.
- Job-board accounts already signed in and any boards the user does not want used.

## Campaign Defaults to Confirm Once

- Posting age: seven days.
- Sources: LinkedIn, Indeed, and direct employer pages.
- Match threshold: prioritize 75 and above; review 60–74 when transferable evidence is strong.
- Output format: ATS-safe DOCX/PDF resumes plus an XLSX tracker.
- Submission mode: prepare for review, or proceed through forms and pause before final submission.
- Storage folder for the master resume, tailored resumes, and tracker.

Never request passwords, one-time codes, or full government identification numbers in chat. Let the user enter sensitive credentials directly into the relevant site.
