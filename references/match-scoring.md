# Match Scoring

Score only after checking hard eligibility. A geographic, authorization, required-license, or mandatory-schedule conflict makes the role ineligible regardless of keyword overlap.

## Weighted Score (100 points)

- Core responsibilities and functional experience: 35
- Required tools, platforms, and methods: 25
- Seniority and years-of-experience alignment: 15
- Demonstrated outcomes and domain relevance: 15
- Remote logistics, schedule, language, and employment-type fit: 10

For each category, award points in proportion to verified evidence. A transferable example can receive partial credit; an unsupported keyword receives zero.

## Interpretation

- 85–100: excellent match; prioritize.
- 75–84: strong match; tailor and apply if no material gap.
- 60–74: plausible stretch; apply when transferable evidence offsets the gaps.
- Below 60: normally skip unless the user specifically wants that role.

Record a one-sentence score rationale and the three largest gaps. Keep the score explainable; never present it as an ATS vendor's proprietary score or a guarantee of interview selection.
